# HLS Action Verbs for Resumes

## Formatting and Usage Rules
* **Tense Consistency**: Use **past tense** for all previous positions.
* **Current Roles**: Use **present tense** only for your current, ongoing role.
* **No Periods**: In accordance with the "clean lines" standard, do not use periods at the end of bullet points.
* **Variety**: Avoid using the same verb (e.g., "Researched") multiple times in one entry; use synonyms to demonstrate a broader range of skills.
* **The Formula**: Every bullet should follow the **Action Verb + Substantive Task + Result/Impact** structure.

---

## Public Interest & Advocacy Categories (OPIA Focus)
*Use these verbs to demonstrate commitment, advocacy, and client-centered service.*

### Direct Service & Client Interaction
* **Represented** clients in administrative hearings or housing court.
* **Advised** or **Counseled** individuals on legal rights and eligibility for benefits.
* **Interviewed** clients and witnesses to collect sensitive information and draft affidavits.
* **Mediated** disputes between stakeholders to reach equitable settlements.
* **Empowered** community members through legal education and "Know Your Rights" workshops.

### Advocacy & Policy
* **Advocated** for legislative changes or policy updates before government bodies.
* **Litigated** complex civil rights or environmental protection cases.
* **Lobbed** for specific interests or community needs.
* **Drafted** pleadings, motions, and appellate briefs for impact litigation.

### Community Organizing & Outreach
* **Mobilized** volunteers or community members for voter registration and outreach.
* **Organized** coalitions of non-profits to address systemic social issues.
* **Facilitated** community meetings and town halls to gather stakeholder input.

---

## Private Sector & Clerkship Categories (OCS Focus)
*Use these verbs to highlight efficiency, analytical rigor, and project management.*

### Analysis & Research
* **Analyzed** complex legal documents, regulatory frameworks, or financial statements.
* **Researched** jurisdictional precedents and statutes to support litigation strategy.
* **Investigated** factual claims and evidence during the due diligence process.
* **Synthesized** high volumes of information into concise internal memoranda.

### Management & Leadership
* **Managed** project timelines and teams to ensure filing date compliance.
* **Supervised** junior staff or interns in document review and administrative tasks.
* **Coordinated** logistics for multi-party closings or international management meetings.
* **Spearheaded** new initiatives or organizational structures to improve efficiency.

### Financial & Technical
* **Evaluated** potential risks in licensing agreements or emerging market ventures.
* **Calculated** cost forecasts and quarterly budget reports.
* **Projected** earnings and market trends for technology and telecommunications clients.

## General Professional Categories
* **Communication Skills**: Addressed, Authored, Briefed, Corresponded, Drafted, Edited, Presented, Published.
* **Organizational Skills**: Categorized, Charted, Compiled, Logged, Monitored, Organized, Scheduled.
* **Teaching & Mentoring**: Coached, Demystified, Guided, Instructed, Mentored, Trained.
